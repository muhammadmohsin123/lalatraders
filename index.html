Company profile

2017

The year our supply network began serving Pakistan's growing infrastructure economy.

Built for the realities of the site.

Lala Arif Traders is a professionally managed construction material, logistics and workforce company headquartered in DHA Phase 6, Karachi.

We support infrastructure, road construction, housing, power and industrial projects across Pakistan. Relationships with leading cement, steel and material manufacturers let us source at project scale without compromising specifications.

Led by Muhammad Arif, our team treats a single delivery and a multi-phase supply chain with the same discipline: clear communication, verified materials and dependable site coordination.
